Spacing

The organization of space is key to every great design. Spatial system provide rules that give the designs a consistent rhythm, constrain decision making, and help teams stay aligned. Uniformity on a spatial level allows your product to be more consistent, the team to communicate better, and reduce the number of decisions designers have to make in a day.

Spacing Scale

Based on our grid system preferred method is an 8 px linear scale for elements and a 4 px scale for spacing icons or small text blocks. Typography will use 4 px baseline which means the line-heights of the font are divisible by four.
In exceptional cases, it is allowed to use no more than two indents in a row.
